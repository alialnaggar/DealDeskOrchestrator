## 1. Research problem and system objectives

Pre-approval deal assessment combines product configuration, commercial calculations, delivery feasibility, customer credit evidence, company policy and approval routing. The [Phase 1B target design](../../phase-1b-agent-architecture/agent-architecture-and-deal-flow.md) requires evidence attribution and human control over decisions. The **implemented research artefact** is a bounded, source-grounded prototype that demonstrates how six specialist responsibilities can be orchestrated, separated into scripted observations and deterministic verification, and reported without treating a proposed delivery change as an accepted deal. The narrower research question supported here is: *Can a reproducible offline workflow preserve subject/evidence identity, verify one candidate and fail closed before human review?* Whether real language-model assistance improves deal outcomes is a **future empirical question**, not answered by the demonstration ([architecture](../architecture/supervisor-demo-d7-implemented-architecture.md), [evaluation](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d7-evaluation-2026-11-09.md)).

## 2. Design-science methodology and implementation approach

**Proposed methodological framing:** treat the governed workflow, its executable contracts and its evidence-preserving UI as a design-science *artefact*; iterate from a documented problem and target design to narrow implementation, verification and reflection. Do not present adherence to a named design-science method or evidence of general utility as established solely by this draft. The [D1 feasibility audit](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d1-offline-feasibility-2026-11-08.md) established a single integrated scripted path, [D2 projection](../architecture/source-grounded-recommendation-projection.md) defined a read-only review boundary, [D3 API](../architecture/offline-assessment-api.md) made that boundary owner-scoped, [D4 frontend](../frontend/README.md) exposed it, [D5 QA](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d5-quality-assurance-2026-11-08.md) challenged failures, and [D6 rehearsal](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d6-rehearsal-2026-11-08.md) refined supervisor interpretation. The D6 tag freezes one tested iteration; D7 records its provenance. Each stage tests design constraints rather than supplying an independent effectiveness score.

## 3. Multi-agent architecture

The architecture assigns separate Configuration, Pricing & margin, Fulfillment, Credit & risk, Policy & compliance and Approval Routing responsibilities to an orchestrated assessment. In this *offline demo*, each responsibility has a deterministic original-subject result and a separately identified **scripted model-protocol observation on V0**; this is not six independent hosted agents running in production. The [actual graph and trust-boundary diagrams](../architecture/supervisor-demo-d7-implemented-architecture.md) show the fixed source pin, specialist execution, bounded Fulfillment investigation, optional candidate verification and read-only projection. A V1 candidate, when present, receives **six deterministic rechecks and no candidate-specific AI reassessment**. The Orchestrator schedules and checks the work; it cannot grant authority or replace the underlying fixed decision ([`orchestrator.py`](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/orchestrator.py), [`assessment_service.py`](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/assessment_service.py)).

## 4. Data strategy and synthetic evaluation environment

The [Phase 1C source](../../phase-1c-data/implementation/phase1c_release_review.md) provides structured synthetic deals, evidence, rule profiles and a read-only pinned verifier. The demo uses the exact separate checkout `90c0bc8e285ee69262c7db826b786096a77ad004`, a new synthetic business PostgreSQL database, and a different checkpoint/registry database. The [setup CLI](../backend/tests/setup_assessment_api.py) installs only allowlisted RC01/B01 and UC02/N01 overlays in fresh disposable fixtures. Test fixtures and synthetic credit records are **not independently accepted specialist answer keys**. In particular, the 36 held-out cases are not ready to support measured accuracy; they remain isolated from the demonstration and unchanged ([baseline](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d7-baseline-2026-11-09.md), [evaluation limits](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d7-evaluation-2026-11-09.md)).

## 5. Agent implementation and orchestration

[`AssessmentService.run`](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/assessment_service.py) invokes a PostgreSQL-checkpointed LangGraph original graph, then offline Configuration/Pricing protocols, bounded Fulfillment inspection/proposal, optional V1 fixed verification, and offline Credit/Policy/Routing observations. The explicit `offline_double` implementations follow model interfaces but do **not** contact a hosted provider. For RC01, the scripted hypothesis is 8 units on 3 September and 2 on 8 September, compared with an original request for 10 by 4 September; the [candidate graph](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/candidate_graph.py) verifies the exact subject, source and changed slices. For UC02, no verified candidate is produced. PostgreSQL checkpoints contain internal state; append-only API events represent coarse persisted transitions rather than token-level or per-model progress. An interrupted run may become terminal `uncertain`; restarting does not resend its reserved or potentially executed action ([architecture](../architecture/supervisor-demo-d7-implemented-architecture.md), [D3 API](../architecture/offline-assessment-api.md)).

## 6. Governance and deterministic verification

The Phase 1C verifier, rather than a model claim, determines fixed findings and checked candidate supply. RC01's revised schedule is **supported but unreserved**; because two units arrive after the original requested date, the candidate remains `needs_revision`, customer acceptance stays `pending` and Routing roles remain withheld (`null`). UC02's fixed Credit is `unknown` because required evidence is stale/unavailable; an original scripted observation marked `completed` does not mean credit clearance. The [D2 projector](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/recommendation_review.py) rejects incomplete/mismatched source, candidate or citation lineage, and the [D3 service](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/assessment_service.py) rereads business evidence when serving a completed review. No endpoint accepts customer approval, reserves stock, waives policy or writes the business source ([D2 contract](../architecture/source-grounded-recommendation-projection.md), [D3 API](../architecture/offline-assessment-api.md)). Human authority is **outside** this artefact, not merely an affirmative UI label.

## 7. Evaluation methodology and actual evidence

For this iteration, use *requirements-to-test traceability*: unit/component tests for DTO interpretation, backend integration/regression on fresh disposable databases, real loopback browser journeys, process/DB/source/checkpoint fault injection, and service-restart checks. Predefined assertions concern identity, event ordering, fail-closed review, authorization boundaries and the two specific business outputs. [The consolidated D7 report](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d7-evaluation-2026-11-09.md) cites each category and its actual passing counts, versions and environment; counts across D1–D6 are repeated regression rounds, **not** a sample of independent deal outcomes. Screenshots corroborate rendered information but do not validate hidden business facts without DTO/source assertions. A future formal study would require independently reviewed expected findings on untouched cases, a predeclared fixed-rule comparator, clear eligibility/exclusion criteria and independent scoring of specialist correctness, grounded alternatives and governance violations. Until then, do **not** report accuracy, precision, recall, improvement, latency or cost for the offline demo.

## 8. Limitations, validity threats and future work

**Internal validity:** scripts and pinned fixtures improve control/repetition but suppress the uncertainty of real model responses; tests share code and source fixtures. Fault injection is selective, not exhaustive. **Construct validity:** a source-verified partial schedule does not mean customer agreement, a reserved delivery, general credit clearance or approval. **External validity:** two allowlisted synthetic deals and a local single-operator HTTP UI do not represent production customers, other policies or open-ended deal inputs. **Security validity:** known residual Starlette and dev-tool advisories and missing public TLS/identity/rate limiting prohibit deployment claims ([D6 audit](../evaluation/supervisor-demo-d6-rehearsal-2026-11-08.md)). The 36 held-out cases have not been independently accepted, so measured specialist accuracy is **unavailable**. Future work is a supervisor-approved answer-key/evaluation protocol, potential broader cases and rule-only comparison; separately governed framework hardening, provider authorization and real-data integration are **not** implicit next steps of this offline baseline. Candidate V2, six candidate-specific AI reruns, automated acceptance/reservation and human sign-off are not implemented. See the [D6 offline runbook](supervisor-demo-d6-offline-runbook.md) for claims prohibited during a live demonstration.
