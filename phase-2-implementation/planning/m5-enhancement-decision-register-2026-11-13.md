## Classification and authority

- **IMPLEMENTED:** already present in the narrow verified offline or gated development contract, not a proposed new feature or a measure of usefulness. Most recent full local Phase 2 regression: **384/384** on eight fresh isolated PostgreSQL databases at the cited baseline; remote CI not verified.
- **DEFER:** potentially useful optional expansion, excluded from this approved milestone freeze unless separately approved by change control.
- **REJECT:** unsafe, unsupported inference or outside pre-approval thesis scope; not a backlog promise. Reconsidering requires a new source/authority decision, not merely more time.
- **BLOCKED:** valuable work that cannot safely execute until a specified external contract/owner/security/cost authorization exists. The classification does not approve that dependency.

**Formal optional-feature freeze, effective November 13, 2026:** No discretionary Phase 2 specialist capability, candidate-search expansion, orchestration optimization, UI enhancement, Pydantic migration or other DEFER item enters the current milestone by default. Defect/regression fixes, security/identity/authorization/audit repairs, testing, documentation, necessary safe thesis-completion fixes, and already-approved core work remain permitted within their own gates. M3/M4 are **not** completed by this decision; M6 paid-provider integration and M7 formal evaluation still require separate authorization and reference-answer readiness. **BLOCKED and REJECT remain binding**: freeze approval does not authorize previously blocked source intake, a Phase 1C change, new infrastructure, paid API usage, a hosted launch or autonomous business action. Any optional post-freeze addition requires a separately documented **owner-approved change-control decision** with justification, research value, risk, cost, dependencies and acceptance criteria before implementation. Nothing retroactively alters pinned fixtures, completed tests, Phase 1C contracts or reviewer authority.
