# M2.1 interim original-only continuation after fixed candidate

**Approved by Ali, 29 October 2026: Option A only.** This is an opt-in
continuation for a fresh synthetic assessment in the guarded Groq development
runner; the existing no-candidate path remains unchanged. Option B (AI checks
for both original and candidate) is **not** implemented or authorized.

The original deal retains its sealed evidence, original six deterministic
results and review status. M1.7 constructs at most one binding Fulfillment
candidate, checks its sources and whole-deal rules through the pinned Phase 1C
adapter, and records six *candidate-subject deterministic* results including
exact-candidate Routing. `supply_supported_unreserved` is not booking,
customer acceptance or approval. A candidate with `needs_revision` retains
withheld Routing and its separate reviewer comparison.

After a fixed-checked candidate and completed candidate reviewer package,
the M2.1 continuation may run its existing original-only Credit, Policy and
Approval Routing **AI evidence investigations**. A distinct, versioned hosted
manifest pins the candidate ID, candidate-input digest and fixed-decision
digest. Preflight and every specialist node verify that marker against the
sealed, pinned fixed decision, all six candidate-subject deterministic results,
original identity and the unchanged candidate comparison. The model never
chooses a subject: its tool identities, returned references, attempts, claims
and `m21_review.subject_id` remain the original deal ID; its wire projects a
stable redacted token rather than the candidate ID. The candidate reviewer
projection remains untouched; only the original AI review is added.

Invalid identity, altered fixed digest, changed reviewer comparison, missing
candidate result or interrupted/terminal model attempt fails closed. The
PostgreSQL checkpoint, exact owner manifest and one-use receipts prohibit
replay. AI observations cannot change fixed Credit/Policy, any candidate
outcome, Routing eligibility or customer acceptance. The original and
candidate still require human review. A completed AI invocation may abstain;
that is not a favorable business finding. Candidate-specific AI checks,
selective reruns and human acceptance of a linked revision remain future work.
