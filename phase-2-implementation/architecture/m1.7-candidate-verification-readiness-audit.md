## The business question and four different statuses

For an illustrative request of 20 units by Friday, the fixed reader can find 12 supportable by Friday and an offer for eight later subject to supplier confirmation. Customer permission to receive partial deliveries permits investigating a schedule; it does not confirm the offer, delivery date, stock allocation, customer acceptance of a revised date, or internal approval. A proposal can be **shape valid** while its supply remains **conditional or unknown**. Fixed verification may establish **binding, unreserved support** for another schedule; this is still not a promise or business approval. Human review is a distinct decision. The illustrative 12+8 conditional split has not passed fixed verification.

### What the 20-unit split can establish today

The Phase 1C `Candidate` structure can express two dated slices totaling 20, with the original date marked unmet and customer partial permission checked. The original reader separately distinguishes binding receipts/stock from supplier offers. The latter are lead-date estimates, not a confirmed arrival or delivery promise. If only the first 12 are in binding `shipping_options`, the cumulative 20-unit reader-backed check cannot establish `supply_supported_unreserved` for the second slice; falsely labeling the offer confirmed or citing it as binding is rejected or stays unknown. The M1.2 tool reports a mixed path as unsupported; M1.6 may safely show the supplier inquiry as a nonactionable planning idea. No fixed confirmation, customer acceptance of a later date, stock reservation, or approved deal follows. The 10-unit binding test demonstrates a *different*, narrower supported case; it cannot be extrapolated to this conditional offer.

## Proposed integration and subject lifecycle (not yet implemented)

1. Preserve the original fixed assessment, immutable submission, sealed evidence context and M1.6 accepted/rejected claim trace. Only a shortage-eligible investigation already permitted by W3.7 may contribute; do not broaden eligibility to obtain more candidate trials.
2. Require a **new, separately versioned, structured AI proposal payload** for a binding partial path before constructing a candidate. It contains original run/subject/line identity, delivery slices (quantities, dates, cited returned-source references), customer-constraint explanation and tradeoff. Model-provided IDs, source status and commitments are untrusted. Ordinary M1.6 conditional `candidate_idea` remains nonactionable; do not silently turn a downgraded idea into a schedule.
3. The trusted Orchestrator checks exact run/subject/version, citations against records actually returned under the ledger, positive bounded quantities, explicit customer permission and proposal budget (initially at most two distinct candidates, one revision level). It allocates candidate ID and parent lineage, builds Phase 1C's existing `Candidate` with exact `FieldChange` and `DeliverySlice`, marks `unverified`, and records raw model proposal and failure reasons. These preliminary checks are integration guards, not a second availability/rules engine.
4. Call the already existing Phase 2 `verify_candidate` adapter. Phase 1C decides shape, dated binding supply and fixed aggregate decision in a read-only transaction tied to the sealed source digest. A digest/identity mismatch halts the affected candidate; no refresh, fallback confirmation or fabricated rerun. Source-not-supported, business rejection and technical errors remain distinct.
5. On a fixed checked candidate, rerun/map all six distinct responsibility results for **that candidate subject** from the candidate aggregate fixed decision, preserving the original results and checking Configuration prerequisites, independent Credit/Policy findings and Approval Routing on the fully checked exact candidate. Full rerun is the W1 conservative baseline; selective invalidation waits for proof. Candidate status is not the same as business status. If fixed verification cannot establish binding supply, retain its reason and do not mark it verified.
6. Build a typed reviewer package: original and each candidate's identity/lineage, exact requested-versus-proposed dates/quantities, fixed and AI provenance, source/commitment/freshness references, confirmed/conditional/unknown/infeasible classification, unmet original date and customer acceptance, six per-subject findings, fixed candidate verification status, rejected/downgraded AI claims, conflicts/errors, route eligibility and known required roles. When Phase 1C withholds roles on a blocked route, show **roles unavailable/not determined**, not an inferred role list. No auto approval, reservation, supplier/customer commitment, or revised submission.

Minimal status progression: `idea_only` (conditional nonactionable) **or** `proposal_unverified` → `construction_rejected` / `fixed_verification_unavailable` / `fixed_checked` → `review_package_ready` or `review_package_incomplete`. Record source digest, subject and attempts at each transition. Resume must preserve original, candidate lineage, remaining budget and returned-record ledger; repeat read-only verification only against the exact seal and deduplicate completed outputs. Model/tool failure preserves the original result.

## Open limitations and approval boundary

- Phase 1C owns any conditional/mixed supply-path verifier, customer-acceptance semantics, blocked-route role extraction, general substitutions or broader multi-line/production verification. Those are **not** Phase 2 business-rule patches. Any later Phase 1C SHA change requires an explicit integration review and approval.
- Re-reading live PostgreSQL rows in the adapter compares against the seal and fails on change; a future immutable dataset replay strategy might be needed for long-lived assessments. Do not claim restart across changed source data works without that mechanism.
- Phase 1C's pinned candidate identity is an in-memory frozen value, not a durable human-accepted revision. Phase 1C candidate tests and sidecar experiments do not establish a full reviewer workflow. Approval Routing roles for blocked subjects and independently reviewed business answers are incomplete.
- This document records **proposed** M1.7 scope, not a new approved decision or evidence of successful implementation. Wait for Ali's approval before code, tests, live calls or Phase 1C work.
