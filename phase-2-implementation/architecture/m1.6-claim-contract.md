# M1.6 Fulfillment claim contract and validation boundary

**Status:** Approved bounded design, implemented for original-deal investigation. No candidate verification or new specialist. Phase 1C remains pinned at `90c0bc8e285ee69262c7db826b786096a77ad004`.

## Why this boundary exists

The revised-prompt baseline on pinned R03 produced valid JSON and a returned supplier-offer citation but proposed 20 units on the customer's requested date. A supplier's lead estimate and offer validity do not establish delivery. We therefore separate model suggestions from factual statements the reviewer may see.

## Version 2 contribution

The provider final turn returns `schema_version=2`, `kind` (proposal, finding_without_proposal, abstention) and a Fulfillment payload containing `line_id`, up to eight `claims` and audit-only free-text `notes`. Runtime assigns trusted run, producer and specialist identities. Each claim has `type, line_id, source_ref, field, value, scope, gap_code, idea_code, delivery_slices`; unused strings are empty and unused slices are `[]`. Exact Python TypedDicts are in `backend/dealdesk_phase2/claim_validation.py`, and the live final JSON schema in `live_models.py`. Earlier version-1 offline contributions remain readable, but their free text is withheld from the validated reviewer summary.

| Claim | Required grounding | Reviewer-facing meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `source_observation` | Exact exposed field/value on a record returned by an authorized tool for this line/context | Directly observed value; explicitly does not establish delivery |
| `fixed_finding_reference` | Exact Fulfillment status or quantity by request date from the pinned fixed input | Phase 1C finding, never a new Phase 2 calculation |
| `conditional_possibility` | Returned supplier offer, explicit supplier confirmation required, no delivery slices | Conditional and uncommitted |
| `evidence_gap` | Selected line supply tool status and `scope=returned_records`, with no pagination loss for selected-pool absence | Bounded gap, never universal absence |
| `candidate_idea` | Returned offer and allowed `ask_supplier_for_later_option` code | Unverified, nonactionable question; dated slice rejected and separately audited |

The 20-unit example: fixed Phase 1C supports only 12 by Friday. The model can quote an actual returned stock count, identify the uncommitted offer and suggest asking the supplier about the remaining eight. It cannot claim Friday delivery or a confirmed later date from the offer's lead estimate. The illustrative 12-plus-eight split is **not** verified at this pin.

## Authority, failure and trace

The validator checks source references against completed tool-ledger returns in the same run, subject, line and evidence digest, rather than checking existence in PostgreSQL. Wrong identity or forged/unreturned citation rejects the entire contribution. Unsupported values or fields are rejected per claim and kept verbatim with a reason in `ai_claim_audit` in the LangGraph checkpoint. An unsupported dated idea is also recorded verbatim as rejected; if its offer citation is genuine, a distinct downgraded, undated and nonactionable planning idea may remain. No silent rewrite.

The review summary comes from renderer templates applied only to accepted claims; free-text notes and raw errors stay in the audit. Even a successfully generated validated claim does not change the six fixed specialist results, the original deal, business approval or candidate verification. The live benchmark reports raw rejected claims and reviewer-facing summaries separately.

This is a deliberately narrow equality and scope boundary, **not** a second Phase 2 Fulfillment rule engine. It does not derive dates or quantities, adjudicate conflicting source semantics, prove the completeness of a bounded pool or decide that a candidate is feasible. The Phase 1C conditional-candidate verifier dependency remains open. The provider final turn uses a strict JSON schema and the same trusted M1.2 investigation tools; no new dependency or technology is introduced.
