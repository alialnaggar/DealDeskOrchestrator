# M2.2 supervisor-signed campaign authorization — trust design for review

**1 November 2026 · specification, not authorization.** Ali approved [Option A as a design direction](m22-campaign-authorization-design-gate-2026-11-01.md), **not** the security parameters, an implementation, a campaign or any provider send. The thesis supervisor is the **proposed**, not yet consenting, independent signer/key custodian. This document is an addendum to the [A/B/C gate](m22-campaign-authorization-design-gate-2026-11-01.md), not a change to the [M2.2 original exit criterion](../planning/master-implementation-blueprint.md#8-remaining-implementation-roadmap-and-gates). The current [structural audit](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/m22_authorization_contract.py) **always blocks**; [prospective hosted launcher](../backend/dealdesk_phase2/m22_hosted_readiness.py) **always refuses**. No real cryptographic verifier, key or hosted campaign is present.

## 1. Principals, capabilities and evidence

| Principal / record | Proposed responsibility and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Supervisor (unconfirmed) | Independently reviews a frozen campaign packet, controls signing key **outside** Ali's VM/accounts and runner process, signs only a reviewed digest/envelope, retains issued approval and revocation/rotation records under their own control. May decline the role; no technical service or credential-management duty can be presumed. A supervisor signature authorizes a *research campaign*, never a business deal or thesis answer. |
| Ali / researcher | Prepares exact case registry, six stage manifests, preflights, budget/window proposal and independent review packet; operates an eventual runner. Can read signed approvals and published keys but cannot sign, change authoritative retained records or publish trusted revocation/key updates. A researcher-writable copy is not the authority. |
| Ali/project security decision-maker | Explicitly approves issuer/role mapping, identity proofing, canonical format/algorithm/dependency, issuance and revocation service levels, data exposure, cost/quotas and later offline integration. A distinct decision is required for any hosted tranche. |
| Prospective runner (not implemented) | Loads a **pre-provisioned independently authenticated** issuer-to-key binding and current independently authenticated revocation state; verifies exact files and accounting at every pre-send boundary. Local clock, VM root, files, environment, credential and callback are not an independent trust root. Full compromise of the runner/host defeats software-only enforcement; deployment integrity/privileged-operator risk needs a separate acceptance decision. |

Supervisor identity must be established by a separately agreed out-of-band process (e.g. an in-person verified key fingerprint plus independently controlled publication channel), *not* by a key attached to an email, Discord message, Git commit or file supplied by Ali. A public key proves only possession of its paired private key **after** the identity-to-key binding has been independently authenticated. No identity provider, hardware key or signing infrastructure is assumed. **Until the supervisor accepts, issuer identity, private-key separation, trusted public-key origin, signing consent and live revocation cannot be demonstrated.** Do not generate a key locally and call it independent.

Proposed custody requirements for supervisor discussion: create/store/backup the private key solely in an environment they control, protect with access controls, never transfer it to this VM/GitHub/Discord, never expose raw keys or source evidence in signing logs, restrict who can sign and revoke, and keep a confidential compromise-reporting channel. Whether they use an existing offline signing tool, organizational key custody or decline technical custody is their choice; key generation algorithm, custody mechanism, recovery, backup and organizational permissions await explicit agreement. The signed artifact, unsigned review packet, approval ID, issuer key fingerprint, time of issuance, immutable digest, signature verification outcome, authorized case IDs, reservations and receipt reconciliation form the auditable record; do not store credentials, private keys or hidden reviewer answers. External retention must be demonstrably write-protected from the researcher, with independently verifiable access/version history and availability for later thesis review; GitHub in an account Ali controls does not suffice by itself.

## 2. Proposed canonical artifact and signing transcript (future version, **not** the current prototype)

Propose `m22-supervisor-campaign-authorization-v1` as a **new future** envelope, not an alias for `m22-campaign-authorization-proposal-v1` and not a silent change to `m22-scheduled-registry-v1`. An independently retained review packet contains the complete frozen registry and **six full immutable stage-manifest byte streams** prepared *before the first request*, plus a human-readable summary. The signed envelope binds:

| Area | Required exact content |
| --- | --- |
| Domain / issuance | Contract version, purpose `m22_prospective_research_only`, unique unpredictable authorization ID, campaign ID, issued-at UTC, not-before UTC, expires-at UTC, supervisor issuer subject and signing-key ID/fingerprint, authorized operator identity, intended provider/account ID and checkpoint namespace. The operator and issuer cannot be the same principal. A signature is not a hosted-execution permit. |
| Frozen cases/source | SHA-256 of the *exact stored registry bytes* (or an explicitly defined canonical re-encoding), **and separately** the existing `m22-scheduled-registry-v1` internal SHA (computed using its existing `_digest` convention); registry version, Phase 1C exact commit `90c0bc8e285ee69262c7db826b786096a77ad004`, Phase 2 code/contract SHA, ordered full case-array digest, counts by cohort and every case/assessment ID, original deal/run, as-of/snapshot and selected-source/bundle digest. Do not assume JCS SHA equals the current internal SHA. No reclassification of 36 held-out/100 diagnostic/supplemental cases or changed result inclusion. Prohibit adding post-review cases or changing evaluation rules via a new side file. |
| Six stage grants | Array of exactly `configuration`, `pricing`, `fulfillment`, `credit`, `policy`, `approval_routing` **in that order**; each binds specialist, stage manifest format/version and canonical SHA-256 of the **full** stage manifest, provider/model/account, trusted prompt version and actual instruction/template bytes hash (and schema/tool/wire contract versions and bytes hashes), per-request max output tokens, stage max posts per case and total, allowed attempt/revision count and stage-specific identity. Dynamic request bodies must be checked against the frozen template/allowed variable schema and registered case before reservation; a stage-manifest hash alone cannot authorize arbitrary payloads. The existing development CLI creates manifests during execution, so its files cannot be retrospectively signed as prospective approval. |
| Resource and run ceiling | Maximum posts per case **and across all six stages/cases**, each stage total and per-case ceiling, per-request token ceiling, aggregate configured output-token ceiling with safe upper-bound accounting, account/rate plan, explicit maximum monetary exposure with currency/billing attestation reference, and a bounded time window. Budget even for failed/reserved attempts; `0 USD` is *not* an enforceable provider cap unless an independently checked account-level billing control supports it. If no credible hard spend bound is available, **no hosted send** pending separate approval. |
| Trust / restart | Revocation-authority/key ID, revocation channel and freshness policy identifier, approval ID bound to one checkpoint namespace and registry/run identity, unique reservation identities, durable one-use start and monotonically consumed budget ledger; no new run namespace or fresh assessment under the same signature. No reset of spent/uncertain attempts on restart. |

**Canonical serialization proposal to approve before implementation:** strict UTF-8 JSON of a versioned, closed schema; RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization Scheme (JCS) is one candidate *if an approved maintained implementation is selected*. Alternatively a precisely specified restricted JSON profile (ASCII field names, NFC or ASCII-only identifiers, no floats/NaN, UTC integer epoch timestamps, integer USD minor units, exact string normalization, reject duplicate/unknown keys) may reduce dependency risk but requires careful independent conformance review. **Do not reuse** `m22_registry._canonical` (`sort_keys` + compact `json.dumps`) as an interoperable signature standard without defining duplicate-key rejection, Unicode normalization, number/time representation and byte limits. Define a signed transcript with a fixed domain separator and length-delimited canonical envelope bytes, e.g. `b"DealDesk/M2.2/supervisor-campaign/v1\x00" + uint64_be(len(body)) + body`; signature field is outside the body. The signed envelope contains algorithm and key ID, which must exactly match a locally pinned allowed issuer/algorithm map; no attacker-chosen `alg`, URL, or embedded public key can create trust. Approve a maintained crypto verifier/library (e.g. Ed25519 is a **candidate**, not adopted) and canonicalization package, with known-answer vectors from the independent issuer; **never implement signature mathematics manually**.

Verification specification: parse with duplicate-key/unknown-field/oversize rejection; canonicalize and require *byte-for-byte canonical input*; verify signature over the exact domain-separated transcript with the independently provisioned key; compare a trusted externally retained record/digest, exact registry canonical bytes and internal digest, all six *full* stage-manifest bytes/digests, pinned Phase 1C/Phase 2 versions, selected case/source/prompt/model/schema/account/quotas, UTC window, issuer-to-role/operator binding and independently authenticated fresh nonrevocation evidence. Verify again immediately before **each** reservation; on file changes, re-read and rehash. The signature does not prove that the supervisor actually examined hidden case evidence, that the provider cannot bill, or that approved reference answers exist. `audit_proposal` only compares mock labels and digests; its `signature_b64` is opaque and it must not be promoted to a real verifier.

**Existing-interface gaps (proposal only):** `StageGrantV1` lacks provider/account, per-stage prompt *bytes* source, template/tool bytes and allowed request substitution schema, aggregate per-stage tokens and billing attestation. The existing registry's `contract_versions` is a case-wide version map, not six stage manifests; `PROMPT_PLAN_SHA256` hashes version labels, not prompt bytes. `OfflineCampaign` uses owner-local files and a case start marker, not an independent signing/root/atomic campaign budget ledger. Existing sender receipts cover only selected transports; Fulfillment iterative transport is not equivalently receipt-audited. Before any implementation, approve whether to introduce a separate signed-envelope version and how to construct each exact stage manifest from existing modules; do **not** quietly expand the pinned registry, change the current proposal audit or weaken its always-blocked result.

## 3. Revocation choices and failure/restart rules

| Revocation option for a small research tranche | Benefits / burden / fail-closed condition |
| --- | --- |
| **Short-window signed status snapshot (proposed minimal offline path)** | Separately controlled revocation authority signs a versioned, issuer-bound status/denylist with issued-at, expires-at, monotonic sequence and authorized key/approval IDs. Runner receives it via an agreed independent channel; checks signature, issuer, freshness and non-decreasing sequence **before each reservation**. Supervisor (or explicitly delegated separate custodian) must publish an initial status and any emergency revocation and make updates available. A snapshot cannot guarantee instantaneous revocation while offline; a missing/stale snapshot or unknown newer generation stops new sends. Restart must retain highest observed sequence in protected durable storage; if rollback resistance against a privileged operator is required, use an external monotonic source or stop—local file modes do not provide it. Required refresh interval/window and acceptable delay are **unapproved**; keep it strictly short and never equate ‘no file’ with ‘not revoked’. |
| **Authenticated online status at each reservation** | More timely revocation if service is available and controlled independently; needs trusted endpoint/identity, network, uptime and an approved external system, and still cannot recall a request already delivered. No such service is approved. Timeout/unknown response stops; do not reuse a stale cached ‘good’ status. Supervisor need not administer it unless they explicitly opt in and its operator is named. |
| **No revocation feed; expiration only** | Simplest operations but cannot reliably honor a mid-window withdrawal. **Rejected for a sendable campaign** unless Ali/security expressly accept a very short, bounded, offline exposure and document the residual risk; expiry itself is still required. |

Rotation: trust-root versions and issuer-to-key mappings are independently published and pinned; authorize overlap **only** for a predeclared bounded interval. New approvals use new key; old approvals may continue only when old key remains explicitly trusted, unrevoked and the approval is still in window. On compromise/unknown provenance, stop all affected campaigns, revoke key **and** affected approval IDs using an independently authenticated emergency channel, quarantine outstanding uncertain sends, reconcile receipts; never retroactively re-sign an active approval or swap key IDs. If no timely trustworthy revocation channel exists, stop and re-authorize under a newly independently provisioned root. Key compromise may make old signatures unverifiable as genuine; retain evidence and require supervisor/security adjudication, not an automatic favorable decision.

| Event | Proposed runner behavior (all future; launcher still blocked now) |
| --- | --- |
| No supervisor acceptance/signature, untrusted/missing key, bad signature, unknown algorithm/issuer/operator, inaccessible external retention | Block *before* credentials, start marker or reservation; no local fallback or Boolean/callback override. |
| Revocation missing/stale, clock/time-source uncertain, expired/not-yet-valid, revoked approval/key | Stop **new** sends before reservation. Existing reserved/uncertain attempts remain spent; append a sanitized stop audit and seek independent status; do not extend the window locally. |
| Registry, case membership/source, stage manifest/prompt/model/account, request-template substitution or budgets differ | Fail closed; a different packet needs new approval ID, campaign, checkpoint namespace and reviewed case identities. Maintain original submitted deal; no candidate/answer leakage. |
| Stage/campaign tokens or requests would exceed reserved ceiling, or account billing cannot be attested | Stop before request; allocate conservatively using configured worst-case tokens for each reservation, not later measured usage; enforce campaign-wide atomic budget under durable cross-process lock/transaction, including failed/uncertain sends. |
| Reservation issued; timeout/crash/uncertain send; process restart | Treat possible provider delivery as terminal uncertainty, keep reservation consumed and never retry that attempt. Reconcile write-once case start, checkpoint, stage receipts and cumulative campaign ledger **before** considering a *different* not-yet-attempted stage/case; if inconsistent/unknown, block whole campaign for human investigation. Never reconstruct a missing ledger from local files the operator can replace as though that proved independent authorization. |
| Supervisor revokes while executing | Stop the *next* reservation immediately upon verified fresh status (bounded delay for signed snapshots). A request already issued cannot be withdrawn; record in-flight/uncertain outcome and do not promise retroactive prevention. Halt if status cannot be checked; supervisor and Ali coordinate incident communication outside model outputs. |

Existing `m22_enablement.OfflineCampaign` write-once start and `hybrid_send_reservation.claim_and_call` exclusive receipt are **local no-replay components**, not a global budget or independent approval. The [WP5 audit](../evaluation/m22-wp5-evaluation-readiness-and-exit-gate-2026-11-01.md), [enablement](../evaluation/m22-evaluation-enablement-2026-11-01.md) and [hosted-readiness gate](../evaluation/m22-hosted-readiness-gate-2026-11-01.md) show why simply wrapping the old `_six_campaign` is unsafe. A future prospective sender must be the *only* authorized route for study traffic, integrate authorization before any send reservation, and demonstrate that the old opt-in development path cannot be misused as a prospective bypass; **no change to historical CLI behavior in this package**.

## 4. Decision gates and minimal supervisor review checklist

**A — Approved by Ali now:** Option A **as direction only**, researcher/operator and thesis supervisor **proposed** as separate roles. This is not consent by the supervisor, approval of parameters, an implementation mandate or hosted authorization.

**B — Supervisor confirmation needed (ask, do not assume):**

- [ ] Are you willing to serve as independent signer/key custodian for a *single bounded research campaign*? You may decline or nominate an approved independent custodian for Ali's separate review.
- [ ] Can you independently verify your identity/key fingerprint with Ali and retain the signed artifact and revocation notices outside researcher-write access? What existing trusted channel/tool, if any, would you prefer? **No new infrastructure duty is assumed.**
- [ ] Are you willing to inspect a human-readable frozen packet (case IDs/cohorts, source SHA, six ordered stages, prompt/model identity, exact request/token/spend ceilings, window/operator) before issuing, and to have a way to withdraw approval? Which contacts and response expectations are realistic?
- [ ] What key-loss/compromise reporting and later audit-retention process would be acceptable, without giving Ali a signing key or exposing private evaluation answers?

**C — Ali/security explicit decisions still needed:** identity proofing/trust-root distribution and privileged-host threat model; custody/rotation/revocation authority and freshness limit; canonical JSON/algorithm/verifier dependency and cost/security/learning implications versus signed snapshot or online status; exact stage-manifest formats and request templates, provider account/billing controls, request/token/spend budgets, operator and execution window; external retention and audit access. Do not install dependencies or provision trust roots until approved. If supervisor declines, return to B/C alternatives in the [prior comparison](m22-campaign-authorization-design-gate-2026-11-01.md), not a self-signed substitute.

**D — Subsequent offline implementation, separately scoped:** publish frozen stage manifests and review packet; implement established-library signature verification against independently provisioned test trust roots, freshness/rotation/revocation interface, durable campaign-wide reservation ledger, request-template checking, issuer/reference conformance vectors, restart and fail-closed fault-injection tests on fresh PostgreSQL. Keep mock credentials/transports, default non-sendable launcher and old development CLI unchanged until a reviewed integration plan. Test missing/bad key/signature, altered bytes, duplicate JSON, stage permutation, wrong model/prompt/account, budget overrun, stale/revoked status, rollback, expiry, concurrent reservations, restart/uncertain send and developer-path bypass; no fixture may turn into provider authorization.

**E — Separate hosted authorization:** after supervisor role/issuance and D are independently verified, Ali must *separately* approve a named, timed, quota- and account-bounded campaign and any real provider charges. Accepted Phase 1C source-first reference answers and compatible-pin decision, complete actual-stage timing, fixed-baseline comparator and human-only reviewer action remain independent original M2.2 evaluation gates. No claim of formal completion follows from a valid signature.

**Example (illustration, not a campaign):** B01's submitted partial-delivery request is read from the frozen Phase 1C source/bundle; a future Fulfillment AI could suggest eight stocked units now and two confirmed inbound units later. The supervisor's signature would cover only the *permission to run a predeclared, quota-bounded investigation*, not that suggestion or a delivery promise. Fixed candidate verification must still rerun all six responsibility results on the exact candidate; the reviewer and customer decide on any change. Existing mock tests cover boundaries/no-replay, **not** the supervisor's signature or business correctness.
