# M2.2 campaign authorization — independently verifiable design gate

**1 November 2026; design and untrusted structural tests only.** Phase 2 branch `phase2-agent-implementation`, Phase 1C pin `90c0bc8e285ee69262c7db826b786096a77ad004`. The [hosted-readiness audit](../evaluation/m22-hosted-readiness-gate-2026-11-01.md), [original M2.2 exit row](../planning/master-implementation-blueprint.md#8-remaining-implementation-roadmap-and-gates) and [prospective evaluation protocol](../planning/m22-scope-and-evaluation-design-2026-11-01.md#predeclared-evaluation-protocol--freeze-before-any-trial) still govern. This document **does not authorize or implement** a real signature verifier, signing key, identity provider, hosted sender, reference-answer acceptance or new provider spend. `run_hosted_prospective` remains permanently non-sendable at this gate.

## What must be approved *before the first provider request*

The existing WP2 `m22-scheduled-registry-v1` freezes campaign ID, Phase 1C/Phase 2 SHAs, every scheduled case ID, cohort, family, original assessment/deal/run, cutoff, source/bundle digests and selected contract/model/prompt versions. Its `sha256` detects changes against an **independently retained** digest but the same operator can replace both. `record_preflight` marks each eligible/ineligible/source-failed case once; eligible does **not** confer campaign authorization. `inspect_prospective_case` checks the pinned six-fixed original and prompt-*version* plan, not all actual prompt bytes, six requests or spending. The old `_six_campaign` constructs the typed Configuration/Pricing, Fulfillment and M2.1 Credit/Policy/Routing **development** manifests as it executes, including local stage approval files; the typed and M2.1 transports check stage identity, per-stage budgets, receipts and no-replay, but their local artifacts are not an independent multi-stage approval. Its historical CLI must not be retroactively registered or reclassified.

An authorizer must approve **the complete canonical scheduled registry and independently frozen six-stage manifest set**, not a case after viewing results. Each stage needs specialist identity/order, exact provider/model ID, wire/schema versions, prompt version **and hash of actual trusted instruction/schema bytes**, per-request and per-case/total POST and completion-token ceilings, exact stage-manifest digest, credential/account selection and source subject. The campaign must cap all posts and configured tokens across cases/stages including failures and reservations, one operator/checkpoint namespace, allowed window, independently attested billing-disabled zero-USD upper bound (or a separate **new** explicit paid-spend decision). Predeclared excluded cases stay scheduled; no eligible case send before validated preflight and authorization. A digest or zero configured USD **does not enforce provider billing**. Human acceptance and reviewer answers never enter model input or the campaign approval payload.

## Alternatives (no existing option is already approved)

| Design | Independent issuer, authentication and authoritative retention | Scope/window/revocation, verification and replay | Complexity, cost/learning and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Independently signed, externally retained authorization artifact** | Ali or an expressly appointed approver signs **outside** the operator's account/VM using a separately controlled signing identity/key. Publish the signed immutable envelope and digest in an approver-controlled repository/record inaccessible for operator writes; provision a **pinned trusted public key/issuer map independently**. The operator must never possess the signing key or mint the approval file. | Signature over canonical versioned envelope binds registry SHA and six immutable stage SHA-256 manifests, exact case-set/cohort/source, operator, budgets, window and approval ID. Runner checks trusted issuer, signature, exact SHA/bytes and fresh signed revocation status before *every* reservation; no stale/missing revocation snapshot may permit a send. Expiry and a short execution window limit offline exposure; for prompt revocation use an independently maintained fresh signed denylist or an authenticated online status check. Approval ID + immutable campaign/run/assessment namespace and write-once registry/receipt checks forbid reuse and uncertain-send replay. A valid signature alone cannot grant approved business authority or change fixed rules. | **Recommended, conditional on Ali's approval of issuer/key custody, verifier trust root, revocation freshness and security process.** Smallest additional runtime surface (local verification), but key loss/rotation, canonical format, release signing and fresh revocation distribution add security/learning/operations effort. No new service *necessarily* required; signing tool/crypto library choices require separate approval. Offline-only verification cannot establish instantaneous revocation without a sufficiently fresh independently controlled feed. |
| **B. Independently controlled approval service / trusted external approval record** | Ali/security governs separate authenticated account/service with role separation, audit and durable approval storage; runner authenticates service response with pinned trust. Operator cannot create approvals. | Service issues single-use campaign authorization bound to all exact manifest hashes and budget; online per-send check validates expiry and immediate revocation, with durable server-side accounting/nonces; unknown service result fails closed and never retries uncertain sends. | Stronger central revocation and account-level quota enforcement, but needs identity provider/service, availability/SLA, network, secrets, deployment and maintenance. **Not approved and unnecessary for a small offline thesis tranche** unless A's independently controlled key/revocation feed cannot be guaranteed. |
| **C. Manual approval + cryptographically bound immutable metadata + separately controlled verification** | Ali reviews a frozen human-readable packet; a *different* trusted custodian writes an immutable approval record using a separately authenticated channel (e.g. protected record with enforced ownership). Operator receives only verifiable read access and pinned verifier identity. A chat/Discord statement or same-user file alone is not this option. | Record binds all six immutable stage digests and case/source/limits. Separately controlled verifier checks authenticated approver, approval version, start/end, revocation and consumed approval ID before send. Without independent signature/access isolation, only hash matching is possible and modification/replay cannot be rejected. | Manual workflow may suit rare campaigns, but demonstrating genuinely separate control and revocation can cost **as much as A**, and a local SHA/file/Boolean is insecure. Alternative only if an already approved independent record+verifier is identified; no such path is established here. |

**Recommendation:** choose A *only after* Ali confirms an independent issuer and key custodian, trustworthy public-key distribution and fresh revocation strategy. Otherwise **stop** and assess B or a demonstrably independent C; none is implicitly selected. A compatible cryptographic primitive (for example Ed25519), verifier library, public-key location and operator identity integration are **proposals** with security/implementation/learning implications, not a technology decision or authorization to install software/keys. No production infrastructure cost or signing API has been approved.

## Versioned typed proposal (not executable authorization)

`backend/dealdesk_phase2/m22_authorization_contract.py` describes `m22-campaign-authorization-proposal-v1` with `CampaignAuthorizationProposalV1` and exactly six `StageGrantV1` records. The fields are: `approval_id`, `campaign_id`, `campaign_manifest_sha256`, `case_set_sha256`, `phase1c_commit`, `phase2_commit`, `cohorts`, stages (`specialist`, `model_id`, `prompt_version`, `prompt_sha256`, `wire_version`, optional `schema_sha256`, `stage_manifest_sha256`, `max_requests_per_case`, `max_requests_total`, `max_completion_tokens_per_request`), campaign `max_requests_total`, `max_requests_per_case`, `max_completion_tokens_total`, `max_provider_spend_usd`, `billing_disabled_required`, `window_start`, `window_end`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`, `operator_subject_id`, *different* `approver_subject_id`, `issuer_key_id`, `checkpoint_namespace`, `revocation_feed_id` and opaque `signature_b64`. The `case_set_sha256` is a canonical digest over the **entire ordered registry case array**, including cohort, assessment/run, exact source/bundle, as-of and contract IDs. The signature must eventually cover the full canonical envelope with a domain separator and exclude only its own signature field. The **exact canonicalization, algorithm, signature format, key rotation and trust-anchor provisioning remain unapproved design details**; the current `_digest` is a structural test convention, not a signature protocol.

Accept only a frozen manifest whose independently preserved digest matches, six unique specialists in prerequisite order, exact stage bytes/signatures and bounded sums/limits; reject missing scope, duplicate assessment, changed catalogue/policy/rule source, swapped line, unsupported cohort, off-window request, unknown/expired/rotated issuer or stale revocation feed. Sign/recheck an exact **pre-send** snapshot/namespace and account-specific billing attestation; compare remaining per-stage and campaign reservation budgets atomically with write-once attempts **before any network call**, not afterward. A reserved/uncertain V1 attempt is spent even if provider delivery is unknown. Never release or reuse its approval ID for a changed campaign, candidate or later assessment; a new campaign needs independent approval, new case identities and acceptance. Preserve PostgreSQL checkpoints and existing bounded receipt verification; unreceipted Fulfillment transport and uncertain delivery must remain explicit unknowns. Human approval and customer acceptance remain separate and pending.

**Offline prototype limit:** `audit_proposal` tests structural scope, model/prompt version, membership, size/positive budgets, zero-spend claim, time, issuer *label* and an untrusted mock revoked-ID set. It **always** returns `authorization_status="blocked"` and `independent_verification_unavailable` even when every synthetic field matches. There is no signer, public key, authenticated issuer/role, authoritative revocation status or way to call a sender. An assertion against a mock issuer is not evidence of real-world issuer authentication or independent approval. The actual sender must not import this prototype as a permission decision. Documentation and tests are intentionally versioned separately from the pinned checkpoint and historical receipts.

## Proposed integration sequence (requires explicit approval first)

1. Independent operator freezes registry and publishes digest for approval; prepare **all six** stage manifests/budgets/prompt/schema fingerprints before any send. Separate immutable human-readable review of cases/cohorts, pins, original/candidate eligibility, quotas and free-tier billing state. Current `_six_campaign` cannot safely be used unchanged as the prospective launcher because it creates manifests as it goes; do **not** change its historical development CLI behavior.
2. Approver issues an externally retained signed record; the prospective launcher independently obtains the pinned trust root, verifies signature/issuer/role, signed freshness/revocation, allowed operator/account/time and exact manifest/case/stage bytes **before acquiring first provider reservation or credential**. Fail closed on unknown identity, stale revocation, clock ambiguity, unavailable record or changed role. No runtime config/default/key or callback can bypass it. Recheck budgets and authorization under a controlled per-campaign lock before each stage request; preflight identity and source pin are checked against the actual six-fixed checkpoint and exact fresh evidence read.
3. Persist a durable immutable approval-to-assessment/run/checkpoint binding and write-once start marker before the first send. Reconcile every reservation and receipt against campaign ceiling, exact stage identity, selected case and stopped/uncertain state; never automatically retry or resume an uncertain send, including after process restart. Stage completion and human review are not provider authorization. Keep scheduled-but-not-started denominators and cohorts in WP2 registry.
4. Recheck the entire gate with offline fake transports, simulated revocation/expiry/concurrency/crash/receipt tampering and clean disposable PostgreSQL. Only after owner/security acceptance and a **separate** explicit quota-bounded hosted campaign decision may a new launcher become sendable. Independent Phase 1C answer acceptance/compatible-pin review remains an additional original M2.2 evaluation gate.
