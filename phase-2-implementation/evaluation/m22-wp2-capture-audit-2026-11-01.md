## WP2 scheduled registry and closure review (offline)

**Contract:** `m22-scheduled-registry-v1` in `backend/dealdesk_phase2/m22_registry.py`. Before any model send, `register_campaign` exclusively creates and fsyncs a single owner-only manifest with stable campaign/case/assessment/original/run IDs, source cohort and family, exact Phase 1C pin, Phase 2 SHA, as-of, snapshot, bundle and source digests, and declared contract/model/prompt versions. The caller keeps the returned SHA-256 out-of-band and supplies it to reconciliation; different/replaced membership fails closed. `record_preflight` exclusively creates and fsyncs a per-case `eligible`, `ineligible` or `source_preflight_failed` marker, bound to the frozen manifest with a digest. A repeated/changed outcome is rejected, not overwritten. The manifest and markers are owner-local offline artifacts; they are *not* tamper-proof against a privileged owner replacing both files and the out-of-band digest. A formal held-out campaign requires separate source-first answer acceptance and an independently preserved pre-execution manifest digest. No answer key or model output enters this registry. The campaign is a caller-invoked capture boundary (not automatically wired into the old hosted runners); old runs cannot be retrospectively registered as prospective formal campaigns.

**Read-only reconciliation:** `reconcile_campaign` reads the frozen manifest/markers plus caller-supplied checkpoint states and optional bounded receipt root. It calls strict `capture_m22` for any started case and `reconcile_receipts` when explicitly requested; pin, original, assessment, run, as-of, snapshot, bundle/source digest and capture contract must match. Wrong/unregistered checkpoints, preflight rejection with a checkpoint, changed manifest, receipt mismatch or changed subject fail closed. No checkpoint and no preflight remain `scheduled_unobserved`; eligible without a checkpoint is `eligible_not_executed`. A checkpoint without a reserved model attempt distinguishes explicit no-send stop from still incomplete no-send. Reserved/unreturned is uncertain; returned-but-error is `attempted_incomplete`, not validated. Six-stage governed completion is *only* the validated checkpoint projection, never inferred from receipts. Case-level stage/category and per-cohort rows remain visible; receipt checks are `not_checked` unless explicitly invoked. Neither helper sends, retries, modifies checkpoint/receipt files or invents provider delivery.

**Exact WP2 denominators:** `scheduled` = immutable manifest rows (including unobserved/preflight failures); `eligible` = explicit eligible preflight markers; `started_eligible` = eligible cases with a stored checkpoint (not a synonym for a provider call); `attempted_cases` = distinct cases with at least one stored *reservation*; `provider_send_reservations` = reserved rows across all four model ledgers (one per attempt, not both reservation and terminal); `returned_responses` = paired returned/completed rows (not necessarily accepted findings); `validated_specialist_outputs` = original specialist completed or valid abstention outputs, never fixed-only or error/skipped; `completed` = checkpoint-verified six-stage governed completions. `failures_by_stage_category` keeps preflight exclusions, no-send stops, uncertain and rejected finals separate. Reports include all case rows and cohort-specific counts; `fault_injection`/`pinned_development` cannot be pooled as independently accepted held-out cases. These counts support the planned completion numerator over **started eligible** and the provider-return numerator over **reserved attempts**; report both and also scheduled/eligible coverage to expose early failures. Accuracy denominator remains all **accepted scheduled** cases once accepted answers exist; no accuracy fraction today. Grounding and safety metrics use parseable submitted claims and attempted assessments respectively per the M2.2 protocol; this registry does **not** claim to extract raw rejected claims. A reservation bounds possible sends but does not prove the provider received it; per-attempt network delivery and full sequential latency remain unavailable without future gated observation.

**Offline failure-control audit:** preflight rejection, eligibility exclusions, scheduled unobserved, no-send stop, reserved-only, returned/rejected versus validated, original/candidate isolation, changed membership and wrong checkpoint/receipt identity: `test_m22_registry.py` and `test_m21_hosted_gate.py` (real six-stage checkpoint/receipt restart). HTTP 400/429, timeout, simulated crash/restart and uncertain Credit: `test_m21_hosted_gate.py`; local quota before send: `test_groq_dev_rate.py`; connection loss and sanitized category: `test_hybrid_provider.py`; malformed HTTP response: `test_groq_http400_offline_design.py`; invalid terminal, fabricated/wrong-source citation and schema: `test_typed_final_hosted.py`, `test_m21_groq_contract.py`, `test_m22_registry.py`; tampered receipt, duplicate concurrent send, unauthorized/uncertain replay: `test_hybrid_send_reservation.py`, `test_hybrid_offline.py`; changed source/subject: `test_m21_hosted_gate.py`, WP1 matrix. These are *offline controls*, not observations of historical hosted failure causes. Unreceipted Fulfillment iterative transport and some M2.0 paths remain explicitly unverified. Missing full sequential wall-clock, usage and cost are null, not zero. No answer/36-case status, pin, business rule or accepted accuracy changed.

**Closure decision:** WP2 *offline engineering* criteria in the ordered plan are met for a predeclared campaign using this registry: identity-bound counts agree with stored checkpoints and bounded receipts; stopped/unknown and early failures remain in the registry; no uncertain attempt is replayed. WP2 is **closed for offline engineering**, not a claim that historical M2.1 runs were prospectively registered or that future formal campaigns have started. WP3 six-specialist evidence-gap implementation is the next package, subject to its separate contract review. Provider reliability, full sequential latency, accepted-answer comparison and Phase 1C release compatibility remain WP5/separately gated evaluation dependencies, not retroactive WP2 blockers.
