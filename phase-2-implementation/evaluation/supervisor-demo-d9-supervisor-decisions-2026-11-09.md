## Six-case pilot: scope, workload and scale gate

The [blank, editable subset](supervisor-demo-d9-pilot-blank.json) selects **001, 005, 009, 020, 023, 031**, from distinct source categories (stock, stale evidence, offer, competing route, digital proof, credit/policy); no model outcome was used. All six packet/provider pairs and their v3 scope hashes were locally verified at D9. This does not confirm that the reviewer has the files or has read/accepted them. Start only after deciding whether source-first judgments may use the v3 Credit scope and what to do with an unresolved gate; use [handbook](supervisor-demo-d9-review-handbook-2026-11-09.md) and source-only index, not this table, for the blind first pass. Preserve a first-pass snapshot before consulting 020 choice or model/author drafts. **Existing 001–004 historical failed second-model attempts must not be replayed** as part of this human pilot.

**Planning estimate, not a measured rate:** allow 10–20 minutes per case for retrieval/hash/context, 30–50 minutes for six findings/arithmetic/citations, 10–20 minutes for first-pass quality check, plus **15–30 minutes after unblinding** for disagreement documentation: roughly **65–120 minutes per case**, **6.5–12 hours for six**, plus 1–2 hours initial setup and a separately scheduled supervisor decision meeting. Unfamiliar reviewers, unresolved 020/Credit questions or missing exports may take longer. Scaling to the other 30 at the same assumed pace is **32.5–60 additional reviewer hours**, plus adjudication; this is budgeting, **not observed performance**.

**Scale only if:** all six source-first first-pass forms have verified hashes, traceable citations for each of the six responsibilities (or explicitly justified evidence limits), real calculations/rationale, honest provenance and preserved timestamps; the reviewer and supervisor can resolve 020/Credit ambiguity without changing a source silently; pilot structural checks pass; the method/second-model checklist and v3 scope-binding intake are approved in the authoritative Phase 1C record. Otherwise retain six drafts as pending and improve the method. Acceptances require separate case-level decisions. Even after 36 author-adjudicated synthetic answers, scoring could support only agreement against **that** accepted set with transparent uncertainty and correlated-case limitations—not independent expert accuracy, real-world generalization, hosted-model reliability or elapsed-time claims without a separately authorized and measured campaign. **No supervisor approval, independent answer, hosted call or measured accuracy is recorded by D9.**
