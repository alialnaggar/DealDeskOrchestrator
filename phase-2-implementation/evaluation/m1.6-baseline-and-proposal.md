## Historical M1.6 design proposal — subsequently approved

Use a versioned typed list of small domain claims, tied to line identity and a specified evidence scope: source observation, referenced fixed finding, conditional option, selected-evidence gap or unverified candidate idea. Claims of binding supply, dated availability or supplier commitment may only reference the relevant fixed Phase 1C finding/path or an exact authorized returned source field; no offer validity period by itself proves delivery. A candidate with an ungrounded date is rejected as a recommendation or downgraded to an explicitly undated, unverified planning idea, preserving the original fixed assessment. A statement that no binding source was **returned** may be validated against the ledger; it must never imply there is no supply anywhere. Free-text explanation is not authoritative over validated structured claims. Exact field names, payload union, renderer and rejection-versus-downgrade behavior remain for review before implementation.

No Phase 1C rule engine is copied. The sealed selected-source pool, signed identity, LangGraph checkpoint/tool and model ledgers, 12 tool calls, 25-record responses, forward horizon, Configuration-first fixed workflow and human approval boundary remain unchanged.
