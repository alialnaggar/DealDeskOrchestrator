**What a supervisor can reproduce independently:** verify tag/commit/lock hashes and separate Phase 1C pin; prepare a fresh matched disposable PostgreSQL pair using the [runbook](../planning/supervisor-demo-d6-offline-runbook.md); start loopback API/Next.js without provider credentials; authenticate; launch each allowlisted scenario; compare ordered events and final DTOs to the [D6 browser test](../frontend/e2e/supervisor.spec.ts). Expect RC01 21 events, V0 `needs_revision`, source-checked but **unreserved** V1 8+2 with pending acceptance and withheld Routing. Expect UC02 20 events, **no candidate** and fixed Credit `unknown` with stale/unavailable evidence. Refresh/restart services and confirm persisted reviews; inject faults **only** under the dedicated [D5 disposable fixture guard](../frontend/e2e/fault-injection.spec.ts). The test counts are previous verified results, not a substitute for a fresh run. No archived local VM/database state, screenshots, provider receipt or private credentials are required to inspect the repository, and screenshots under `/tmp/opencode` are **not** portable Git artefacts.

**Not reproducible or supported by this package:** a hosted model's choices or quality, independently scored specialist accuracy on the unaccepted 36 held-out cases, production deployment or a human-approved deal. The dated [D0 plan](../planning/supervisor-demo-plan.md) contains proposed hosted and broader workflows: its planning checkboxes are **historical**, not the implementation status of this offline baseline. The D7 architecture, D5/D6 records and current tagged code govern what may be claimed.
