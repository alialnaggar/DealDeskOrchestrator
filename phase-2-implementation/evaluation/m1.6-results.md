## Source-first expectation

The pinned R03 reference contains a supplier offer without a binding commitment. Its lead time and validity window do not establish delivery on the requested date. Fixed Phase 1C review is `needs_evidence`. A grounded output may observe exact returned fields or note supplier confirmation is required; it cannot promise dates, assert confirmed supply or claim a verified alternative. Pinned R01 has binding supply and skips AI. The synthetic 20-unit, 12-plus-eight example is an offline contract/fault case, not a verified pinned proposal.

Before the new contract, revised-prompt [baseline run 39492127609](https://github.com/alialnaggar/DealDeskOrchestrator/actions/runs/39492127609) emitted a conditional 20-unit proposal on the requested date without a fixed delivery path. ## M1.6 validation method

The model's final JSON uses the five claim types in [the contract](../architecture/m1.6-claim-contract.md). A fixed validator compares references only with returned authorized tool records and compares fixed-finding references with the pinned Phase 1C input. It does not recompute fulfillment. Reviewer summaries are generated from accepted claims; free text stays in checkpointed audit. Every rejected claim keeps its original content and reason. A dated offer idea is rejected as dated and may leave only a distinctly downgraded, undated, nonactionable question. Forged citations and identity mismatches reject the whole contribution. We count raw rejections separately from rejected assertions that reach the validated reviewer summary.

[Routine CI 39494263932](https://github.com/alialnaggar/DealDeskOrchestrator/actions/runs/39494263932) passed 62 tests. The follow-up [CI 39494608405](https://github.com/alialnaggar/DealDeskOrchestrator/actions/runs/39494608405) passed **63 tests** with pinned Phase 1C and disposable PostgreSQL. M1.6 regression tests cover false stock/fixed values, invented arrival field, forged citations and line identity, bounded absence, dated-idea rejection and explicit downgrade, accepted observations and conditions, and final schema mapping. The PostgreSQL case interrupts after a returned tool call; on restart it preserves the evidence context and rejected original dated claim without a second tool-budget charge. Synthetic offline and fault-injection tests are separate from pinned real business references.
