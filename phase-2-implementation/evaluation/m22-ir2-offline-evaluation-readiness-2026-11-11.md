## Reused implementation, actual bounded additions

- **Prospective offline runner:** `OfflineCampaign.freeze/preflight/execute_offline/report` already enforces immutable local membership, exact case/source/model/prompt contract, write-once start, checkpoint and optional receipt reconciliation with mock callbacks. The new `report` accounts separately for **started without checkpoint**: status `started_without_checkpoint_unknown_send`, `started_eligible` increment, failure-stage/category and cohort-specific count only when the durable start marker exists without a checkpoint. It does **not** invent a reservation or provider delivery (`attempted_cases` remains zero without checkpoint proof). Previously available counts still distinguish scheduled, eligible, no-send, reserved-unreturned, returned/rejected, validated, failed and completed; retained timed sidecars keep `null` for interrupted missing wall measurements. Not a sendable hosted runner.
- **Measurements:** existing `OfflineCampaign` monotonic `wall_ms` brackets the offline callback and reconciliation, including measured orchestration/checkpoint work and any waiting **inside the callback**; `MeasuredIntervals` optionally measures explicitly bracketed specialist/graph/review/wait spans. `timing_observation` retains provider-reported elapsed/tokens per returned attempt and `null` for unmeasured stage, wait, checkpoint, totals/cost. It cannot extract six individual stages from three combined development spans or reconstruct a crashed callback's elapsed. No new timing instrument or synthetic latency was added in this increment.
- **Scoring:** existing `score_campaign` requires source-first `independent_accepted` answer *status*, exact case membership/source digests and an answer-set digest. It is a **structural assertion, not authentication of the reviewer**; drafts/provisional answers fail. An optional, separate **post-run only** `m22-post-run-adjudication-v1` record now supports all six specialist semantic/evidence-quality labels, an overall assessment and distinct raw-versus-surviving critical governance codes. Its identity/digest, model-blind/independent-source-check attestations and valid codes must match the accepted answer set and an observed case; missing judgments remain missing. `adjudication_authentication=not_verified`, `formal_accuracy=null` always. Neither labels nor answers are passed to specialist/runner code. A real independent reviewer must supply and authenticate accepted judgments and reconcile disagreements before formal metrics. This interface is deliberately not a score of synthetic accuracy.
- **Matched fixed comparator:** `evaluation_m18.pinned_baseline` remains the exact pinned read-only `discover_partial_delivery` + fixed verification; it cannot represent every six-specialist AI action. The scorer now rejects a supplied baseline with a different case/run/deal/source/bundle **even when comparison is marked excluded/unsupported**. Missing, excluded, unsupported and presence-only comparisons remain distinct; a matching candidate *presence* is not equal quality or schedule. Held-out and diagnostic cohort counts remain separate; synthetic cases cannot be pooled into formal held-out denominators.

**Illustrative deal, not evaluated accuracy:** B01's selected evidence may support eight stocked units plus two confirmed inbound units at a later date. An AI idea for two deliveries is not a commitment. Fixed Phase 1C must check the exact candidate and rerun all six candidate responsibilities; customer acceptance and human approval stay pending.
