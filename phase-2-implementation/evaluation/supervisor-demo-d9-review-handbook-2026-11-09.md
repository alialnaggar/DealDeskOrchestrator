## 2. Make and record a first-pass judgment

For each case, inspect the **original submitted quantity, line configuration, customer/requested date and as-of cutoff**, then select relevant rows and rule IDs. Distinguish product/BOM compatibility (Configuration); exact unit price/cost, discount and margin arithmetic (Pricing); free stock after allocations, confirmed versus cancelled receipts, uncommitted offers, workshop sequencing/capacity, digital term/verified provider proof, service region and shipping timing (Availability); dated credit profile/commitments, AR/payment source and *scope completeness* (Credit); applicable commercial/policy rule IDs (Policy); and conditional routing roles (Approval). Compute/recheck quantities, dates and precedence explicitly. An offer is not a purchase order; source-selected empty AR is not proof of a complete customer ledger; a route is not a human approval or reservation.

The [blank pilot JSON](supervisor-demo-d9-pilot-blank.json) is an **editable subset of six rows from the authoritative Phase 1C blank v2 form**. Copy it into a private reviewer-only location. Its exact `findings` keys are `Configuration`, `Pricing`, `Availability`, `Credit`, `Policy`, `Approval` (the Phase 2 label “Fulfillment” maps to Phase 1C **Availability**, “Approval Routing” to **Approval**). For each finding, fill `finding`, `rationale` (show calculations, conflicting facts, source age, unknowns and alternative interpretation) and resolvable `{ "document": "packet"|"provider", "pointer": "/source_rows/..." }` refs; `Credit v3` pointers are **not supported by the v2 form** and must be versioned by Phase 1C before they can support final acceptance. Fill `overall.decision_status`, `human_actions`, `rationale` and `unresolved_questions` (include **open disagreements** here). Add reviewer `{identity, method, reviewed_at, independence_statement}` with a timezone-aware ISO time and honest author/model assistance disclosure. `pending` is blank; use `draft_complete` only after completing a source-first draft and verifying exported evidence. The v2 allowed finding codes are `no_issue_identified`, `issue_identified`, `evidence_needed`, `human_review_required`, `not_applicable`; gates are `blocked`, `needs_revision`, `needs_evidence`, `needs_commitment`, `approval_required`. There is **no v2 abstain code**: if even a justified finding cannot be stated, keep the case pending, write the gap privately and request a versioned method decision—never invent a sixth code or mark an incomplete case accepted.

**First-pass checklist:** [ ] original deal/context matched; [ ] packet/provider/scope hashes and cutoff checked; [ ] six responsibility findings, counterevidence and exact source pointers; [ ] arithmetic and business-gate precedence with human actions; [ ] absence/freshness/unknown explained rather than silently interpreted; [ ] reviewer identity/time/assistance disclosure; [ ] unresolved alternatives recorded; [ ] no draft answer/prediction opened. Preserve the first-pass JSON and its SHA-256 before comparison. The Phase 1C `freeze_initial_review.py` command applies to its *own* workbook format, **not automatically to this JSON subset**; use that authoritative workflow when operating the Phase 1C Excel register rather than claiming D9's JSON is an equivalent signed intake.

## 3. Compare only after freezing; do not auto-accept

After a frozen first pass, an accountable reviewer may separately read the existing author/model-assisted drafts and technical matrix, record exact agreements/disagreements, new evidence and corrections, and decide whether to defer, reject or request a source-version change. Do not overwrite the first pass. A separate, explicitly approved Phase 1C **v3 scope-bound** intake is needed before a Credit-v3 case can be called accepted. The existing v2 validator may check v2 form structure and citation pointers, but cannot validate v3 scope citations or judge whether a conclusion follows from a cited record. D8's read-only transition check requires a preserved reviewed predecessor before an accepted disposition; it never marks a case reviewed. If the method is author-led, label conclusions *author-adjudicated*; do not call them independent expert ground truth.
