The categories cover (1) how agents communicate and are orchestrated, (2) how business decisions, constraints, process rules, and CPQ are handled, and (3) how agent outcomes, traces, and failures are evaluated. Two additional source texts (Symbolic Guidance and Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?) are cataloged in the index but awaiting upload approval. The source texts and analysis are distinct: a paper conversion is evidence, while the notes record interpretation and citations. The draft is a working submission document; review each claim against its original paper before final submission.

The record retains historical evidence IDs for traceability. Filenames are descriptive titles. The older submission drafts and Phase 1B/1C working files are outside this Phase 1A set.
