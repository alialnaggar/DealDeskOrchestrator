# Phase 1C — reference-scenario dry run

**28 September 2026.** This checks the internal logic of the proposed 24-table calibration with deliberately constructed facts. Fourteen controlled cases in `phase1c_reference_fixtures.yaml` now pass `validate_phase1c_reference_fixtures.py`. This does **not** prove generated data, SQL, the agent implementation or independently authored evaluation fixtures are correct. All example quantities, hours, amounts and dates below are synthetic. The source of applicable parameters is `calibration_config.yaml` (proposed V2 operational values, baseline commercial profile).

## Worked cases and required findings

| Case / controlled input | Deterministic check | Required finding |
|---|---|---|
| A. Finished SKU has 20 on hand and 12 allocated; one confirmed receipt has 5 due and 2 allocated. Customer needs 10. | Free stock = 8; free inbound = 3; 8+3=11. Stock supports 8 now; only the remaining 2 are supported by inbound *after* its receipt and lane transit. | Do not call all 10 `available_now`; report 8 now and a dated balance only if the confirmed receipt, evidence, dispatch and customer arrival all fit. No double subtraction from other generated deals. |
| B. Ten assembled servers: 2 finished units free, 8 to build. Each new unit needs 2 of one component; 9 component units free in stock, 7 more confirmed/free by the build day. Workforce for the assembly step: setup 1 hour + 0.5 hour/unit. Day 1 has 16 available and 12 already allocated; day 2 has 16 available and 8 allocated. | Component need = 16; supply = 9+7=16. Required workforce = 1+8×0.5 = 5 hours. Day 1 free = 4 hours; day 2 free = 8 hours. Check equipment and later operations separately, then add shipping. | Day 1 assembly fails. Day 2 may be `feasible_uncommitted` once all other requirements fit; never label the 8 new units reserved or confirmed. |
| C. An offer can deliver 20 components in 10 workdays, but no confirmed PO/receipt or binding evidence exists. | An offer or planned receipt is excluded from free committed inbound. | Provide a labelled conditional procurement option; no confirmed supply/date claim. |
| D. A 12-month licence quote has 60 full-term user-months, `units_per_period=5`, and requests a supported region/edition/term. Exact pool: 10 user entitlements, 4 allocated, lead 2 workdays, fresh binding evidence for the whole term; requested activation in 3 workdays. | Concurrent requirement = 60/12 = 5. Pool free = 10−4 = 6. Lead 2≤3. | `confirmed_by_date` for activation if evidence is valid at run `as_of_at`. If the commitment is provisional instead, `conditional`; if missing/stale, `unknown`. Billing 60 user-months must never consume 60 simultaneous seats. |
| E. Stock becomes ready Friday after local 15:00 cutoff. The lane dispatches Monday to Friday and needs 2 transit workdays. Customer requests Tuesday arrival. | Next dispatch Monday. Transit days Tuesday and Wednesday give earliest Wednesday arrival (subject to region calendar). | Flag Tuesday as unsupported and propose Wednesday; merchandise being ready Friday is not a Tuesday delivery confirmation. |
| F. A product can be delivered but the requested installation has no matching product/region eligibility rule. | Shipping and installation are separate findings. Missing authoritative eligibility is `unknown`; an explicit exclusion is `ineligible`. | Do not infer that physical delivery confirms installation; request confirmation or an eligible alternative. No installer date is claimed. |
| G. Inventory snapshot is 30 hours old when stock confirmation requires ≤24 hours. | Snapshot age 30>24. | Stock availability is `unknown` pending refresh, never treated as zero stock or available stock. A separate documented supplier option can be `conditional`. |
| H. Customer credit limit €100,000; as-of unpaid AR €30,000; unbilled other commitments €20,000; quote €60,000. No double-counted invoice, no hold. | Exposure after quote = €110,000; over limit = 10%. Baseline exception maximum is 15%. | Credit flags an exception; Approval routes to Finance Director if other checks pass. A hard technical block cannot be waived by that route. |
| I. Server-category product list price €10,000, quoted price €9,000 and standard cost €7,000, with no other flags. | Discount = 10%. Baseline routine maximum is 10%; strict routine maximum is 5%; lenient routine maximum is 15%. | Baseline/lenient routine, strict sales escalation (all other deal facts unchanged); the same immutable deal is replayed under three run policy sets. |
| J. One build has €1,100 BOM materials and 5 workforce hours at €30/hour; overhead is 10% of material plus direct labor. | Direct labor €150; overhead €125; cost roll-up €1,375. | An assembled product's stored standard cost should match within the proposed 5% tolerance; a cost that yields an unacceptable margin must be corrected or generate the appropriate pricing finding, not concealed. |

## Cross-file reconciliation completed

- All four edited Phase 1C files in the project folder were checked against their current Google Drive IDs, names, parent folder, modified times and byte sizes. The project progress file had not yet reflected the new proposal and is updated in this task.
